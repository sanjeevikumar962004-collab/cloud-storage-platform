Frequently Answered Inquiries

Zero-knowledge means that your data is encrypted and decrypted locally on your client hardware using a key derived from your passphrase. The encrypted ciphertext alone reaches our cloud. Even under court subpoena or system intrusion, no one—not even STACKLY system administrators—has the mathematical ability to read your stored contents.
Our primary sovereign infrastructure is located in Salem & Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, paired with distributed edge caching nodes across 40+ global locations. Business and Enterprise customers can lock their storage clusters strictly to Indian borders to ensure 100% compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
Standard cloud drives sync entire files every time a byte changes. STACKLY uses delta-chunking: when you change a single frame in a 40 GB video or a single line of code, our sync daemon transmits only the specific altered 4 MB blocks over parallel UDP/QUIC streams directly to the closest edge point of presence.
